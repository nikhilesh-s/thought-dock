# Demo script (about 3 minutes)

**Setup before presenting**
- Laptop: open the live site `/desk` in a fresh browser profile. It creates a dock with demo content.
- Phone: keep `/capture` ready but **unpaired**.
- Turn the laptop volume on for the /brag video.

| Time | Do | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Play `/brag` (about 40 s) | "Students carry dozens of loose thoughts a day. Most tools make you organize them right when you're busiest." |
| 0:40 | On the desk, click **Pair phone**. Scan the QR code with the phone | "No account. Scan once and your phone is linked to this desk." |
| 0:55 | Phone: type "Scholarship app due Nov 30" → **Save** | "One field, one button. Nothing to decide." |
| 1:05 | Phone: paste a link → **Save**. Then hold **Mic** and say "Remind me to email Professor Lee" | "Voice notes are transcribed right on the phone." |
| 1:20 | Desk: the captures appear in the Inbox within a few seconds | "Later, when you sit down to focus…" |
| 1:30 | Click **Sort**. On the deadline press **T**, pick a date, Enter | "One thought at a time. The suggestion says it looks like a task. I confirm it; it never decides for me." |
| 1:50 | On the link press **M**, type "Eng", Enter | "Move to the Engineering space." |
| 2:00 | Press **A** on one item, then **⌘Z** | "Everything is reversible. Archive isn't delete." |
| 2:10 | Open **Settings**: switch to Dark, turn on **Reduce motion** | "It follows the system theme, and motion can be turned off completely." |
| 2:25 | Tab through the action bar with the keyboard; show the focus ring; press **?** | "Every action has a visible label and a key." |
| 2:40 | Open **Tasks** and **Calendar** → **Add to Calendar** | "Events go into any calendar app as a standard .ics file." |
| 2:55 | Open `/jared` | "The whole design process is documented here." |

**If the network fails:** the phone keeps captures in a local queue and shows "Offline, will sync when you're back". The desk works from its cached copy.
