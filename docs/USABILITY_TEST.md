## Setup
- **Participant:** one student who is not on the team. Write down their year and major only; no
  names.
- **Devices:** their own phone (or a team phone) for capture, and a laptop open to `/desk`.
- **Before starting:** pair the phone using the QR code on the desk, so pairing isn't part of the
  test. Note how long pairing took separately if you want to measure it.
- **Moderator:** reads the task aloud and gives no hints unless the participant is stuck for more
  than 60 seconds. Write down every time you had to help.

## Task (read exactly)
> "Capture an application deadline and useful link on your phone. Later, turn the deadline into a
> task and save the link in an Engineering space."
